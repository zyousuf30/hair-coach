What you'll get

  • Your hair type, from 1A (straight) to 4C (tight coils)
  • Pictures of your type, so you can check it matches your hair
  • A wash-day routine that fits your hair and your schedule
  • Product suggestions based on what you already use
  • Tips for frizz, dryness, flakes or curls that don't last

How it works

  1. Answer questions about your hair, with pictures to compare.
  2. Tell us about your current routine.
  3. Get your hair profile right away, and switch types if another one fits better.
  4. Open your hair guide for product brands and step-by-step hairstyles.
